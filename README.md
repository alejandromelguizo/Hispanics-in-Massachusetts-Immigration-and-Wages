# Hispanics-in-Massachusetts-Immigration-and-Wages
continuation of Econometrics final project, adapting it to my work here in the Gaston Institute. 

# DATA
Data is from IPUMS, CPS data from 2000 - 2025 including 37 variables, and multiple variables constructed in data cleaning files. 

# Methods
Filtered data to include only those observations who are immigrants, defined as observations with birthplace outside of US and territories, and observations with data on "Age at Immigration".
Also filtered to observations within Massachusetts, and Hispanics. 

Inflation adjusted wages with 1999 CPI to put wages in 2000 USD values.

After these filters ~900,000 observations were culled down to ~2,000. 

  # Regressions
  Planning on running regressions on region of origin, educational attainment, sex, age, age at immigration, years since immigration, and other miscellaneous control variables, to see which factors have a statistically significant effect on wages.
